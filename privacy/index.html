AstraCore — Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how AstraCore may process information when you use the Discord application.

1. Information We May Process

Depending on enabled features, AstraCore may process Discord user IDs, server IDs, channel IDs, role IDs, message IDs, server configuration, moderation settings, verification settings, ticket information, leveling data, economy data, giveaway data, reminders, custom commands, and other information required to operate enabled features.

2. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide AstraCore features, save server settings and user progress, maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot problems, and operate the service.

3. Third-Party Services

Some AstraCore features may use third-party services such as AI, music, hosting, database, or other APIs. Information required for those features may be processed by the relevant provider.

4. Data Retention

Information may be retained while it is required to provide the relevant feature, maintain security, operate the service, or meet applicable obligations.

5. Data Deletion

For questions or requests concerning deletion of information associated with a Discord account or server, contact the AstraCore project owner through the official AstraCore support/contact channel.

6. Security

Reasonable measures are used to protect information handled by AstraCore. However, no internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Changes

This Privacy Policy may be updated when AstraCore's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.

8. Contact

For privacy questions or data requests, contact the AstraCore project owner through the official AstraCore support/contact channel.
